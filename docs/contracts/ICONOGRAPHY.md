Read 2026-11-15. ICON-SET was read at 0.41 (section 27 in full); ICON-RENDER at 0.25 is unchanged since its read of 2026-11-14, as section 27 states. Adopted by the owner's decision of 2026-11-13 (S0022): the apps are tied to the family by contracts, and the phone glyphs were ported from FastMediaSorter Android with names that are in the vocabulary. `ICON-EXTERNAL` (pictures from outside) is not adopted: neither app shows a third-party mark, an album art or a downloaded favicon. Both contracts are `draft`, owned by FastMediaSorter Android.

What this repository owes:

- `ICON-SET` section 2 rule 1 and section 6 rung 2: every glyph the product shows before a System information group maps to a vocabulary id, or is a provisional meaning filed by proposal. The table is `docs/design/tokens.json` (`glyphs`); the check is `tools/design/check-tokens.ps1`, which reads the catalog's `vocabulary.jsonl` and fails when a mapped meaning is missing or its Android reference differs from the drawable.
- `ICON-SET` section 15 (a product may add a meaning and ship it): the meanings the vocabulary lacked were filed in `PROPOSAL-2026-11-14-deviceinfo-system-info-meanings.md` beside the contract, accepted with corrections into 0.41 as `proposed` records (section 27, 2026-11-15) - `system.hardware`, `system.memory`, `device.watch`, `system.health` and `system.environment`. The phone ships `ic_sysinfo_hardware` and `ic_sysinfo_memory` provisionally under the proposed ids; the watch ships `ic_watch` (a byte copy of the reference product's drawable, ledger row), `ic_sysinfo_health` and `ic_sysinfo_environment` (the product's own drawings, judged on a preview render only) for its Device, Health and Environment headers (S0035). The three glyphs of the restricted build (`ic_sysinfo_permissions`, `ic_sysinfo_signature`, `ic_folder_open`) are named `notShipped` in the table and are proposed when that build ships. Section 15 does not excuse a glyph-less member among siblings that carry one: that is `ICON-RENDER` 0.25 rule 1, below.
- `ICON-RENDER` section 3 rule 2 (colour comes from the theme): the phone tints a group glyph with a theme attribute, the watch tints it through its `Icon` with the palette role of the same name; neither file carries the colour. `ic_compass` is a multi-colour drawing that both apps tint to one colour, as the phone always did.
- `ICON-RENDER` section 3 rule 9: the watch shows the same glyphs as the phone, coloured from its own palette by the same roles (the identity tokens, `docs/design/IDENTITY.md`); the application icon is an adaptive icon with a monochrome layer in both modules.
- `ICON-RENDER` section 25 rule 1 (0.25: a sibling set draws a glyph on every member or on none): met in source since 2026-11-14 (ticket S0035), so the registry row no longer reads `partial 0.25`. The watch System information section headers were the one mixed set: six drew a glyph (App, Memory, Storage, Wi-Fi, Phone, Sensors), Device, Health, Environment and the Help row at the end of the same list drew none. The owner chose a glyph on all ten headers (rule 4): `WearSectionIcons` maps all nine report sections and the Help row, the three watch-only meanings are proposals (section 15, above), Help takes `app.help`, and `tools/design/check-tokens.ps1` fails when a title string the watch draws as a header has no glyph. The rendered headers are an open check of the device run (S0007); nothing was built or seen on a watch. The phone System information overflow menu is not a mixed set (S0035 inventory, 2026-11-14): the item that turns notifications on has no glyph, but it lives in the overflow popup, which draws titles only because nothing forces optional icons (`menu_system_info.xml`), and rule 2 reads such a popup as a set without glyphs; the toolbar is a second set and all five of its actions carry one. The device observation of the popup is open (S0007). The phone group headers conform: all 24 groups carry a glyph (`SystemInfoGroupIcons`).
- `ICON-SET` 0.40 adds six `proposed` meanings (section 26); none of them is mapped by this product. Every mapped meaning still resolves in the 0.40 vocabulary, and its Android reference equals its drawable (`check-tokens.ps1`, exit 0, 2026-11-14).
- `ICON-SET` 0.41 (2026-11-15, additive, section 27): the five meanings of this product's own proposal enter as `proposed` records, with the 20 usage rows recorded (item F) and the three companions of `ICON-RENDER` section 25 rule 5 pointed to (item H). Re-read against the tree on 2026-11-15, nothing in the product changes: the path data of each drawing it ships is found verbatim in the proposal the records come from (`ic_sysinfo_hardware`, `ic_sysinfo_memory` - the phone's and the watch's equal -, `ic_sysinfo_health`, `ic_sysinfo_environment`, compared by a string search on 2026-11-15; `device.watch` is a reference to the existing `ic_watch` and adds no drawing), and no string, drawable or token has to move.
